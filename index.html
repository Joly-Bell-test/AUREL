Praise of Nature’s Beauty

AUREL — Tribute 1 Green

Forest-inspired tones bring a fresh aesthetic to the most elegant AUREL collection.

AUREL celebrates the richness of the timeless presence of nature with the Tribute 1 Green, a refined dress watch that evokes the deep greens of forests and fields. The off-centre main dial, in forest green with a hand-engraved grain d’orge guilloché pattern and printed white Roman numerals, is complemented by a frosted subdial that creates a richly textured evergreen backdrop.

In balance with this display, the openworked motor barrel is secured by a finger bridge in stainless steel — echoing the case and embodying AUREL’s fusion of mechanical craftsmanship and refined symmetry.

— AUREL, Manufacture Biel

Chapter 01

The Dial

The off-centre main dial, in forest green with a hand-engraved grain d’orge guilloché pattern and printed white Roman numerals, is complemented by a frosted subdial that creates a richly textured evergreen backdrop.

“Near-black shadows transition into vibrant and more intense green hues as light shifts across its surface, yet all elements on the dial remain remarkably legible.”— Escapement Magazine

  • Roman numerals
  • Frosted subdial
  • Rhodium, polished hands

Chapter 02

The Motor Barrel

The openworked motor barrel is secured by a finger bridge in stainless steel — echoing the case and embodying AUREL’s fusion of mechanical craftsmanship and refined symmetry.

“Flaunting the virtues of its manual-winding movement, the barrel is engraved in relief with ‘One Hundred Hours of Power Reserve’.”— Monochrome Watches

  • Openworked barrel
  • Mirror-polished finger bridge
  • Patented equal force barrel

Chapter 03

The Case & Strap

Measuring 38 mm in diameter with a crown at 2 o’clock, the stainless steel case is paired with anti-reflective sapphire crystals front and back for optimal visual clarity.

A matching green Alcantara leather strap with off-white stitching completes the composition with understated elegance, secured by a stainless steel folding clasp.

  • 38 mm × 9.38 mm
  • Crown at 2 o’clock
  • Sapphire, front & back
  • 5 ATM

Chapter 04

Calibre AMW21

The Tribute 1 Green is powered by the manual-winding Manufacture Caliber AMW21, offering an impressive 100-hour power reserve. This is achieved through the innovative architecture of the patented equal force barrel, positioned strategically to optimize space and efficiency.

With a frequency of 25,200 vph and 97% of components produced in-house, it remains a true expression of independence.

  • Manual-winding
  • 135 components
  • 21 jewels
  • 25,200 vph
  • 97% in-house

Forest-inspired tones bring a fresh aesthetic to the most elegant Armin Strom collection.

Armin Strom celebrates the richness of the timeless presence of nature with the Tribute 1 Green, a refined dress watch that evokes the deep greens of forests and fields. The off-centre main dial, in forest green with a hand-engraved grain d’orge guilloché pattern and printed white Roman numerals, is complemented by a frosted subdial that creates a richly textured evergreen backdrop. A matching Alcantara leather strap completes the composition with understated elegance.

In balance with this display, the openworked motor barrel is secured by a finger bridge in stainless steel – echoing the case and embodying Armin Strom’s fusion of mechanical craftsmanship and refined symmetry. Measuring 38 mm in diameter with a crown at 2 o’clock, the stainless steel case is paired with anti-reflective sapphire crystals front and back for optimal visual clarity.

Compact power, pure performance.

The Tribute 1 Green is powered by the manual-winding Manufacture Caliber AMW21, offering an impressive 100-hour power reserve. This is achieved through the innovative architecture of the patented equal force barrel, positioned strategically to optimize space and efficiency. With a frequency of 25,200 vph, the movement reflects the brand’s deep technical expertise – and with 97% of components produced in-house, it remains a true expression of independence.

A modern classic, finished by hand.

Refined in design yet rooted in tradition, the Tribute 1 Green is grounded in the values of traditional haute horlogerie. Every detail is carefully considered: the case features contrasting brushed and polished surfaces, finished entirely by hand. The signature finger bridge is bevelled and mirror-polished to reflect light from every angle. Inside, the movement is adorned with traditional decorative techniques – from Geneva stripes to circular graining – applied with care by skilled artisans in-house.

True to Armin Strom’s commitment to excellence, each Tribute 1 Green is assembled twice – ensuring every piece meets the highest standards of both performance and aesthetics.
